Cookies and analytics

Short, because there is little to say: this site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

On this page
  1. 1. This website
  2. 2. The app and the live demo
  3. 3. If this changes

1. This website

  • No cookies. This site does not set any cookies.
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers. There is no analytics service, advertising network, social-media button or tracking pixel on this site, and nothing is loaded from other companies' servers. The fonts are served from this site.
  • Nothing saved in your browser. This site does not store any setting or item in your browser.
  • Server logs. Like every website, our server records the address, time and page of each request, and your browser's name. We use it to keep the site working and to spot abuse, and keep it for 30 days.

2. The app and the live demo

The app and the live demo do not set cookies either. They keep two preferences in your browser, as the same kind of single setting: your chosen colour theme and the last business you looked at (allevia.theme and allevia.tenant). They stay in your browser.

3. If this changes

If we ever add analytics or anything that sets cookies, we will say so on this page first, tell you what it measures and why, and ask for your consent before it starts where the law requires it. Our Privacy Policy covers everything else we collect.
