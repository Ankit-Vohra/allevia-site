  • Gmail

    Reads
    The emails you allow it to read.
    Acts
    Sends an email from your address, only after you say OK.

  • Google Calendar

    Read and act

    Your team sees when you're free and books calls with your clients on your calendar, after your OK.

    What it can do
    Reads
    When you are busy or free, and today's meetings.
    Acts
    Adds a meeting to your calendar, only after you say OK.

  • Google Analytics

    Pauses or changes an ad, only after you say OK.
