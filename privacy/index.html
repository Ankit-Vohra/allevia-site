The short version

  • We read your public website, and whatever you choose to share: questions, photos, numbers and connected accounts.
  • Each business is its own separate database. We never mix businesses, never sell your information, and never use it to train AI models.
  • An AI model (Claude, from Anthropic) reads what it needs to answer. Sensitive details are removed from text first.
  • Connections to Instagram, Google Search Console, Google Analytics and Google Business Profile are read-only, and are being built.
  • Ask for a copy, or for everything to be deleted, by email. We do it within 30 days.

This summary is for convenience. The full text below is what applies.

1. Who we are

Allevia is operated by Allevia AI, with its registered office at Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India ("Allevia", "we", "us"). You can reach us about privacy at ankit@allevia-ai.in. Our person responsible for privacy requests and complaints is Harjinder Singh Vohra.

We play two roles. For what a business gives us to run its team (its website, photos, numbers, connected accounts, chats), we process the information on that business's behalf and under its instructions, and the business decides what to share. For our own website, contact requests and billing, we decide how the information is used.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Allevia website at allevia-ai.in ("the site"), the Allevia app, the public demo, and the Allevia server and bot on Discord (together, "the service"). It does not cover other sites we link to, or the platforms you connect, such as Instagram, Google and Discord, which have their own policies.

4. What we do with it, and what we do not

We use information to run the service for the business it belongs to: to read your site, answer your questions, draft work for your approval, check facts, write your notes and keep the service secure and working. A person at Allevia may open your workspace to set it up and to help you.

  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not use one business's information for another business. Each business is its own separate database.
  • We do not use your information to train AI models. See Who handles it for us.
  • We do not post, reply or change anything in your accounts without your OK on that exact item.

Our reasons under privacy law, where it applies: to perform our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running and securing a business-to-business service, your consent where we ask for it (for example, when you connect an account), and legal duties such as tax records.

In line with those requirements:

You can stop this at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. We then stop reading immediately, and you can ask us to delete what we already hold (see Delete your data).

6. Instagram and Meta data

When you connect your business's Instagram account, you let Allevia read information about that account. This is being built, and it will be turned on only after Meta has approved Allevia and this page is current.

  • What we ask for: read-only access to your account's profile details and posts (instagram_business_basic) and to its insights, such as reach and follower numbers (instagram_business_manage_insights). We do not ask to read your messages or comments, and we do not ask to publish.
  • What we use it for: to show how your posts and account are doing, to learn which of your posts worked, and to write better drafts for you. We use it only for your own account, only to provide the service to you.
  • What we do not do: we do not sell Instagram data, share it with data brokers, use it for advertising or to profile people, or use it for any other business. We do not use it to train AI models.
  • Publishing: Allevia does not post to Instagram today. If we add posting, we will update this page first, and a post will go out only when you approve that exact post.
  • Who else sees it: the AI model that writes your answers receives the numbers it needs for a question, see Who handles it for us.

You can remove Allevia at any time in Instagram, under Settings, Apps and websites. We then stop reading, and you can ask us to delete what we already hold. The steps are on Delete your data, which is the address to give Meta as our data deletion instructions.

7. Photos and videos

Studio turns a phone photo into post options for your approval. This is how a photo is handled:

  • On arrival, the photo is re-saved without its hidden details, so the location and device information inside it are removed. Videos you send to the Studio channel are kept as they are, and where our server can, their location and device details are removed too.
  • It is kept in a folder that belongs to your business alone, and a photo is only shown to the business that uploaded it. It stays until you ask us to delete it or your workspace is deleted. We do not delete photos automatically today.
  • It is looked at by an AI model, Claude, which is how the Studio decides what to straighten, what must not change and what to write. The photo is sent to Anthropic for that, see Who handles it for us.
  • People in photos. We keep people in your photos as you uploaded them. Each business can switch on a "blur faces" setting in the Studio, and faces are then blurred in the copies we make. We do not use photos of people to identify anyone, and we do not train any model on them.
  • Children. We do not apply AI edits to photos that appear to include children. Those get a simple clean-up only: straightening, brightening and cropping.
  • Number plates and house numbers that we can see are blurred in the copies we make.
  • AI edits are labelled as AI-edited in the app, and a check compares each edit against your original and holds back an edit that added or removed something. Live AI picture edits for customers are not switched on yet. When they are, the photo will also go to our image-generation provider, listed below, and we will tell you first.

You are responsible for having the right to upload every photo and the permission of anyone shown in it. See our Terms of Service.

8. Discord

You can talk to your team in Discord. Each business gets its own private area on our Discord server, and only people with that business's role, our staff and our bot can see it. The bot reads what you write in your business's channels, so it can answer, and it posts waiting approval cards and Today's note there. We store the questions and answers in your business's database. We do not write Discord names or ids into that database.

What you write in #support is read by a person, and a copy goes to a channel only our staff can see, so someone can answer it. Messages and files are also kept by Discord under Discord's privacy policy. The bot does not answer direct messages.

12. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about your business (we send your business's memory and your photos as files),
  • correct anything that is wrong,
  • delete everything we hold about your business,
  • stop using or restrict some of it, or object to how we use it,
  • move it to another provider, and
  • withdraw a connection or a permission you gave us.

Email ankit@allevia-ai.in. We will confirm we received your request within 7 days and complete it within 30 days. It is free. We may ask you to confirm you are the business's owner first, so we do not hand your data to someone else. Today these requests are handled by a person, not by a button in the app.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you more: for example the UK and EU GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, Australia's Privacy Act, the UAE's data protection law and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. We honour these requests whichever of those places you are in. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, and we would like the chance to put it right first.

If you are one of our customer's own customers, and your details are in something your business gave us, please ask that business first: it decides what it shares with us, and we act on its instructions. You can also email us and we will pass your request on.

13. Children

Allevia is for business owners and their staff, who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Photos that appear to include children are handled as described under Photos and videos.

14. Security

Each business is its own separate database, sensitive details are filtered from text before it is stored or sent to the AI model, photos lose their location, and nothing is posted without your OK. No system is perfectly secure, and we are not SOC 2 audited. What we do, and what we do not claim.

15. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when what we do changes, and change the date at the top. If a change matters, we will tell you by email, or in the app or Discord, at least 14 days before it takes effect.
