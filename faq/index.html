Questions. Short answers.

What is Allevia?

Your Founder OS. One company brain remembers everything about your business, and an AI team works inside the tools you already use.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You paste your website and we do the setup with you, so you never face a settings screen.

How long does setup take?

About two minutes. You paste your website, we read it and set your team up, then you connect the tools you want.

Which tools does it work with?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Analytics, Google and Meta Ads, Instagram, Shopify, Stripe, HubSpot, Slack and more. Reading is instant. Anything that sends or spends waits for your OK.

Will it post something that embarrasses me?

Nothing goes out without your OK. Once posting is switched on for your business, a post you approve goes out at the time you chose. Until then it waits as a draft. Replies to reviews of three stars or lower, price claims and anything that spends money never go out on their own.

Is my data mixed with other businesses?

No. Each business has its own separate database, and nothing learned from one is used for another.

What if it does not work for me?

You get a 14-day full refund. If we miss what we promised in the first month, you also get the remaining two-thirds of the three months back. You can ask for a free Brain Snapshot first, before you pay anything.

Will it replace my marketing person, or guarantee growth?

No to both. We promise the setup and the work delivered, never revenue or growth.

What can it not do yet?

Connecting your own tools is switched on one business at a time, and anything that sends or posts is saved as a draft until we turn it on for you. Each person on your team does not have their own login yet.
